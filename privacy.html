Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Who we are

Eventora is operated by [Yash Raj Sinha / CSE AI/ML 3rd year undergraduate student at School of Computer Engineering, KIIT University]. You can contact me at [24155595@kiit.ac.in].

What we collect

When you register for an event we collect your name, email address and phone number, the event you chose, and the time of registration. If you are an organiser, we also store your login email. We do not collect payment card details.

Why we collect it

  • To reserve your seat and prevent duplicate bookings.
  • To send you a booking confirmation email.
  • To let event organisers contact you about the event you registered for.

We use your information only for these purposes, and only with your consent, which you give by submitting the registration form.

Who sees your data

Event organisers on Eventora can see registrations for their events. We use trusted service providers to run the site: Supabase (database and login), Resend (confirmation emails) and our hosting provider. They process data on our behalf. We do not sell your data.

Storage on your device

Your browser stores your theme choice and a copy of your own tickets (so the "My Tickets" section works). This stays on your device and you can clear it from your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep registration details until [for example, 90 days after the event], then delete or anonymise them. You can cancel a registration yourself using your booking ID and email.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent, by emailing [contact email]. We will respond within a reasonable time. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and stored in a database with access rules that stop the public from reading registrations. No system is perfectly secure, so please tell us if you suspect a problem.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
